Big storylines colliding in Asia today — a historic Beijing final, more Shanghai carnage, and a title race getting tighter by the day. Here's your catch-up.

The biggest story of the day comes from Beijing, where home favorite and 2024 Olympic champion Zheng Qinwen will try to make more history in the China Open final. No Chinese player has ever reached this final before — now she's the one standing in the way. Her opponent, fourth seed Mirra Andreeva, is already locked into a career-high world No. 3 no matter what happens today. One of them walks away a WTA 1000 champion; the other still leaves Beijing better off than she arrived.

Meanwhile in Shanghai, Carlos Alcaraz kept rolling, breezing past Juan Manuel Cerundolo 6-3, 6-3 in just over an hour. He's been in sensational form since defending his Tokyo title, and the draw keeps opening up for him — next he'll face whoever survives Zizou Bergs vs. Ignacio Buse.

Daniil Medvedev had to work harder for his spot in the third round, dropping the opener before firing 21 aces and dialing in 88% of his first-serve points to see off Jan-Lennard Struff. Vintage Medvedev chaos, vintage Medvedev recovery.

Alexander Zverev also moved on, and the stakes keep rising for him specifically — a run all the way to the semifinals in Shanghai would clinch him the year-end world No. 1 ranking outright, with Jannik Sinner still out with a knee injury. His next test: Frenchman Quentin Halys.

And don't overlook Felix Auger-Aliassime, who survived a genuine rollercoaster against Camilo Ugo Carabelli, dropping the first set before closing it out 4-6, 7-6(1), 7-5.
