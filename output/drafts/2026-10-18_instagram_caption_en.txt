🎾 TENNIS NEWS YOU MIGHT'VE MISSED TODAY 🎾

Finals Sunday in China — two massive title matches today, plus a big shake-up in the race for Indian Wells. Here's everything you need to know. 👇

It's an all-Spanish affair in the Shanghai Masters final. Alejandro Davidovich Fokina pulled off the upset of the week, stunning newly-crowned year-end World No. 1 Alexander Zverev 5-7, 6-4, 6-3 in Saturday's semifinal. Now he faces 20-year-old sensation Rafael Jodar, who dispatched Daniil Medvedev 6-3, 6-4 to reach his first-ever Masters 1000 final. One of these Spaniards is about to have the best week of their career.

Meanwhile in Wuhan, history is already guaranteed. World No. 170 Sonay Kartal outlasted Jelena Ostapenko in a pair of tiebreakers to become the lowest-ranked player ever to reach a WTA 1000 final. Her opponent? Maja Chwalinska, who shocked an injured Iga Swiatek 7-5, 6-1 in the first-ever all-Polish semifinal on tour. Whatever happens today, a massive underdog story gets written.

Speaking of Swiatek — her early exit is a big blow to her WTA Finals hopes. Elina Svitolina has already locked up the seventh of eight spots at Indian Wells (Nov. 8-15), and Karolina Muchova now has a near-unassailable lead over Swiatek for the final automatic berth.
