🎾 TENNIS NEWS YOU MIGHT'VE MISSED TODAY 🎾

The Asian hard-court swing is heating up and the year-end No. 1 race just got even juicier. Here's everything you need to know 👇

Alexander Zverev kicked off his China Open campaign in style, battling past Cameron Norrie 7-6(1), 6-4 in Beijing. With Jannik Sinner still sidelined by injury, every round Zverev survives chips away at the gap atop the ATP rankings — and he knows it.

Big news off the court too: the WTA just announced a genuine milestone. By 2027, every combined WTA 1000 event will pay men and women equally, with the Italian Open the final holdout to fall in line behind the Canadian and Cincinnati Opens. A huge step for the sport.

Over in Tokyo, Carlos Alcaraz didn't have it easy — he saved three set points before grinding out a 7-6, 4-6, 6-1 win over Alex Michelsen. The reigning Japan Open champ is chasing something special: nobody has defended this title since Pete Sampras in the '90s.

Speaking of Tokyo, today's round of 16 brings a rematch of the Cincinnati final as No. 8 Frances Tiafoe and No. 9 Arthur Fils go toe-to-toe again. Two of the game's most explosive ball-strikers, back at it.

Back in Beijing, Alex de Minaur — fresh off knocking out Zverev at the Laver Cup — takes on big-serving Quentin Halys for a spot in the quarterfinals.

And at the Asian Games in Nagoya, it's an all-China gold-medal final: Wang Xiyu, who stunned top seed Alex Eala in the semis, faces veteran Zhang Shuai for the title today.
