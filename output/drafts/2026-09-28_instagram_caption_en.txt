🎾 TENNIS NEWS YOU MIGHT'VE MISSED TODAY 🎾

Big Sunday for tennis, and we've got you covered before the new week of Asian swing action kicks off.

🏆 Team Europe are Laver Cup champions again! A dominant 13-5 win over Team World in London, sealed when Alexander Zverev got the better of Learner Tien. After the disappointment of losing the trophy last year, this is a huge statement from the Europeans heading into the off-season.

🇨🇿 On the women's side, Czechia lifted their 12th Billie Jean King Cup title, sweeping Ukraine 2-0 in Shenzhen. Karolina Muchova and Linda Noskova — who met in the Wimbledon final back in July — both delivered when it mattered most for their country.

👀 The China Open draw is shaping up to be one of the best of the year. Novak Djokovic is back in Beijing for the first time since 2015, joining a field led by Alexander Zverev with Jannik Sinner still on the sidelines. This one is going to be must-watch tennis.

🇷🇺 Daniil Medvedev keeps rolling in Hangzhou — he ground past Coleman Wong to book a semifinal spot against Roman Safiullin today.

🇬🇪 And in Chengdu, Nikoloz Basilashvili's underdog run is the story of the week. After knocking out Jenson Brooksby, he takes on Alejandro Davidovich Fokina in the semis, while Hubert Hurkacz and Denis Shapovalov battle it out on the other side of the draw.
