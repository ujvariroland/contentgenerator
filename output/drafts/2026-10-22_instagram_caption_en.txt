🎾 TENNIS NEWS YOU MIGHT'VE MISSED TODAY 🎾

From a farewell tour refusing to end to a stacked day of knockout tennis across three continents — here's your tennis fix for today.

David Goffin just isn't ready to walk away yet. In the first round of his very last tournament, the Belgian legend clawed back from a set down and 0-3 in the decider to beat lucky loser Remy Bertola in Brussels, with the crowd roaring him on every step of the way. "I'm really happy that tomorrow we do it again," he said with a smile afterwards — and today he's back out there against fifth seed Alex Michelsen. One more day of magic, at least.

Over in Kazakhstan, it's round-of-16 day at the Almaty Open. Defending champion Daniil Medvedev keeps chasing his title defense, while on the other side of the draw, Karen Khachanov and Tomas Machac — two seasoned top-50 regulars — go head-to-head in what should be one of the tightest matches of the week.

France's brand-new Lyon ATP 250 is more than just a nice stop on the calendar this week — it's a legit ATP Finals audition. Top seeds Flavio Cobolli and Taylor Fritz, plus Tommy Paul, are all fighting to boost their case for Turin, and today's round of 16 could be season-defining for at least one of them.

And on the WTA side, the Ningbo Open quarterfinals are locked in: Leylah Fernandez takes on Ekaterina Alexandrova, while Sara Bejlek faces Marie Bouzkova, both matches set for Friday. Two genuine 50/50 battles to watch.
