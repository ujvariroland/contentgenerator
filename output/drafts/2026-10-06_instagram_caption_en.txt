🎾 TENNIS NEWS YOU MIGHT'VE MISSED TODAY 🎾

Buckle up, because Beijing just delivered one of the wildest finishes of the season. Here's everything you need to know 👇

Daniil Medvedev is OUT of the China Open in the most bizarre way possible. Trailing Novak Djokovic 7-5, 5-3 in their semifinal, he smashed a loose ball in frustration and accidentally hit a spectator in the face — an automatic default. Medvedev was shaken afterward, saying "Terribly sorry to the spectator, first of all. I'm trying to get in touch with him, if he wants it." Brutal way to go out, but it sends Djokovic to today's final with an almost mythical 33-0 record at this event.

And Djokovic won't be alone on that stage. Alex de Minaur is through to face him after Hubert Hurkacz was forced to retire with an adductor injury while trailing 6-4, 3-2 — a heartbreaking end to Hurkacz's breakout week, but a golden opportunity for de Minaur to be the one to finally end Djokovic's perfect Beijing run.

Meanwhile in Tokyo, Carlos Alcaraz is into the Japan Open final after clawing back from a set down to beat Jaume Munar 5-7, 6-3, 6-1. Waiting for him: Jiri Lehecka, who beat Valentin Vacherot 6-4, 6-2 to reach the biggest final of his career.

And there's no rest for anyone — the Shanghai Masters main draw starts Wednesday, with Zverev, Alcaraz and Djokovic all in the same field for the first time since Indian Wells in March.
