Novak Djokovic is turning back the clock in Beijing. The 38-year-old battled past home favorite Bu Yunchaokete 4-6, 7-6(2), 6-2 to reach the China Open quarterfinals — and he's now an almost unthinkable 31-0 at this event. Father Time clearly didn't get the memo.

Meanwhile, Alexander Zverev has a trickier task on paper but a huge prize on the line: beat Chinese wildcard Shang Juncheng today and he'll set up a blockbuster quarterfinal against Djokovic, all while still chasing Jannik Sinner for the year-end No. 1 ranking.

Over in Tokyo, 22-year-old Arthur Fils pulled off one of the upsets of the week, knocking out No. 2 seed Frances Tiafoe 6-3, 7-5. It's only the second time since 1992 that two top-10 players have clashed this early in a tournament — wild stat.

On the women's side in Beijing, world No. 1 Aryna Sabalenka wasn't messing around, cruising past Renata Zarazua 6-1, 6-3 with 11 aces to reach round three.

It wasn't as smooth for Mirra Andreeva, who dropped the first set to Chinese wildcard Yue Yuan before digging in and turning the match around to survive and advance.

And today's big one to watch: World No. 1 Carlos Alcaraz takes on Italy's Matteo Arnaldi in Tokyo as he keeps defending his Japan Open crown.
