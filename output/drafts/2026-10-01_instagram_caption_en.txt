Upsets, title races and a mouth-watering exhibition lineup — the Asian swing is delivering on every front. Here's your daily catch-up 👇

Karen Khachanov is making a habit of beating Felix Auger-Aliassime. The Russian cruised past the China Open's second seed 6-3, 6-3 in Beijing in just over 70 minutes without even facing a break point — his second win over the Canadian in a month, and another blow to FAA's ATP Finals hopes.

Talk about an upset. Alejandro Tabilo absolutely steamrolled seventh seed Tommy Paul 6-1, 6-2 in only 74 minutes at the Japan Open, snapping a three-match skid and picking up his first-ever main-draw win in Tokyo.

Novak Djokovic knows exactly what he's walking into today. The Serbian faces home wildcard Bu Yunchaokete in the China Open second round and expects an "electric atmosphere," joking that "facing a Chinese player in China isn't so much fun" — but he's backing himself after grinding past Nuno Borges to open his Beijing campaign.

Alexander Zverev begins his own Beijing bid today against Cameron Norrie, a man he's beaten all eight times they've played. The top seed keeps one eye on the scoreboard and one eye on Jannik Sinner in the year-end No.1 race.

And mark your calendars — the Six Kings Slam field is set. Jannik Sinner defends his title in Riyadh (Oct 21-24) against Carlos Alcaraz, Novak Djokovic, Alexander Zverev, Alex de Minaur and Taylor Fritz, with $6 million on the line for the winner.
