🎾 TENNIS NEWS YOU MIGHT'VE MISSED TODAY 🎾

Big results, bigger implications — here's what's shaking up the ATP and WTA tours today.

The China Open in Beijing just delivered one of the biggest shocks of the season: brand-new world No. 1 Elena Rybakina — fresh off her US Open title — was stunned in the second round by 118th-ranked Alina Charaeva, 3-6, 6-4, 6-3. Since rankings began in 1975, only eight players ranked outside the Top 100 had ever beaten a reigning world No. 1. Charaeva is now the ninth. Wild.

Meanwhile the race for the ATP year-end No. 1 spot just got spicier. Jannik Sinner's knee injury has now cost him both Beijing and Shanghai, and if Alexander Zverev keeps performing, he could overtake Sinner for the top ranking as early as October 19. That would be a massive turnaround in the season's closing stretch.

Speaking of Shanghai — the Rolex Shanghai Masters, the ATP's last Masters 1000 of the year, is underway, and the draw is loaded with drama. Zverev and Djokovic landed in the same half, setting up a potential blockbuster down the line, while No. 2 seed Carlos Alcaraz opens on the other side against Juan Manuel Cerundolo or a qualifier.

Back in Beijing, the women's quarterfinals are rolling on with both top seeds already gone. 2024 champion Coco Gauff, Iga Swiatek, and Mirra Andreeva are all still alive and fighting for a title that's wide open after Rybakina's and Aryna Sabalenka's early exits.

And don't sleep on Djokovic's Shanghai bid — he's chasing a record-tying fifth title there (his first since 2018), even as his ATP Finals spot in Turin hangs in the balance.
