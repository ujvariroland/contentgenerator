The Shanghai Masters is heating up and the road to Turin just got even wilder. Here's what you need to know 👇

Alexander Zverev survived an absolute rollercoaster against Alexander Bublik — 7-6(6), 4-6, 7-6(1) — somehow coming out on top in a third-set tiebreak thriller. With Jannik Sinner out for the rest of the year, every round Zverev wins pushes him closer to finishing 2026 as world No. 1. Next up: Alex de Minaur in the quarterfinals.

Speaking of de Minaur — he's into the last eight too, beating Karen Khachanov to set up that blockbuster clash with Zverev on Thursday. Two in-form players, one spot in the semis, and a year-end ranking race hanging in the balance. This one's circled on our calendar.

And the upset of the week belongs to Alejandro Davidovich Fokina, who knocked out Hubert Hurkacz — yes, the same Hurkacz who'd just ended Djokovic's perfect head-to-head run against him days earlier. Davidovich Fokina now faces Arthur Géa for a semifinal berth. Shanghai just will not calm down.

Meanwhile over in Wuhan, the WTA's final 1000-level event of the season is underway. Top seed Aryna Sabalenka got a first-round bye and is waiting to see whether home favorite Zheng Qinwen — fresh off her maiden WTA 1000 title in Beijing — can get past American Ann Li. If Zheng wins, that's a mouthwatering second-round date with the world No. 1.
