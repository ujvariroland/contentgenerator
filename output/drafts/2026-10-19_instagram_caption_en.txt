🎾 TENNIS NEWS YOU MIGHT'VE MISSED TODAY 🎾

Two huge champions were crowned over the weekend, and the tour has already moved on to a brand-new week. Here's everything you need to know. 👇

Alejandro Davidovich Fokina finally has his Masters 1000 trophy. After losing his first five tour-level finals, the Spaniard came back from 1-6 down to beat countryman Rafael Jodar 1-6, 7-6(5), 6-4 in Shanghai — his biggest career title, and one that sends him to a career-high ranking.

In Wuhan, an even bigger fairytale played out. 20-year-old Maja Chwalinska won her very first WTA title... at the 1000 level. She beat Sonay Kartal, the world No. 170 who became the lowest-ranked finalist in WTA 1000 history, 2-6, 6-4, 6-2. An incredible story on both sides of that net.

Speaking of fairytales — Jodar's run to the Shanghai final wasn't just about the trophy. It also pushed him past Novak Djokovic and Alex de Minaur in the Race to Turin, putting him on track to qualify for his first-ever ATP Finals as a 20-year-old.

And there's an emotional one too: Belgian fan-favorite David Goffin begins the very last tournament of his career today, as a wildcard at the European Open in Brussels, where he'll be joined in the draw by Felix Auger-Aliassime and Arthur Fils. Meanwhile the tour fans out further — Daniil Medvedev defends his Almaty Open title, Lyon returns to the calendar on hard courts, and the WTA heads to Ningbo and Osaka.
