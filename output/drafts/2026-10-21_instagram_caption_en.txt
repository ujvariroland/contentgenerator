From a tear-jerking farewell to a white-knuckle race for the last Finals spot — here's what's happening in the tennis world today.

David Goffin walks onto court for the very last time as a pro today. The former world No. 7 chose the European Open in Brussels for his farewell tour, and his first-round opponent is no pushover: Swiss lucky loser Remy Bertola, who's already shocked a seed in qualifying. Whatever happens, expect an emotional atmosphere in Brussels.

Meanwhile the ATP's European indoor circuit is in full swing with THREE 250-level events happening at once. On the WTA side, Elena Rybakina heads to Ningbo as defending champion and world No. 1 — but she's clinging to a lead of under 500 points over Aryna Sabalenka in the year-end No. 1 race. No pressure.

And here's the one to watch: the battle for the LAST spot at the WTA Finals in Indian Wells is razor-tight. Karolina Muchova currently leads it without even playing this week, while Iga Swiatek and Marta Kostyuk both need big results in Ningbo just to catch her. This could go right down to the wire.
