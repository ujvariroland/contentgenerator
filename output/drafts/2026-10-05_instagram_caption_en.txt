Novak Djokovic is making history in Beijing. Today he faces Daniil Medvedev for a spot in the final — two former World No. 1s, one incredible stage.

Alcaraz vs. Fritz for the title tomorrow — remember, Fritz has recent bragging rights after beating him at the Laver Cup.
