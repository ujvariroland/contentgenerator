🎾 TENNIS NEWS YOU MIGHT'VE MISSED TODAY 🎾

Draw day in Asia, and two more champions crowned before the week is out — here's everything you need to know.

🇪🇸 Meanwhile in Chengdu, Alejandro Davidovich Fokina battles Hubert Hurkacz for the title, after the Spaniard fought back from a slow start to beat qualifying surprise package Nikoloz Basilashvili.

📅 And keep an eye on the calendar — qualifying wraps today in both Beijing and Tokyo, with the China Open and the Alcaraz-defended Japan Open both throwing open their main draws on Wednesday.
