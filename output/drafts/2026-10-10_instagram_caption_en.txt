🎾 SHANGHAI MASTERS SHOCKWAVES: TODAY'S BIGGEST TENNIS STORIES 🎾

Novak Djokovic looked unstoppable fresh off his China Open title in Beijing — but Hubert Hurkacz had other plans. The Pole ended an 8-0 head-to-head curse in brutal fashion, beating Djokovic 6-4, 6-3 to send the four-time Shanghai champion packing in the second round. Three days, two completely different stories.

As if one shock wasn't enough, world No. 277 wildcard Zhou Yi delivered the upset of the week, grinding past former top-5 star Lorenzo Musetti 7-6(7), 3-6, 7-6(1) in a two-and-a-half-hour battle. He's now the third-lowest-ranked player ever to reach the Shanghai third round — and he gets Hurkacz next. Fairy tale continues?

Top seed Alexander Zverev wasn't about to let the chaos swallow him too. He fought off an injury scare to beat home favorite Wu Yibing 6-4, 7-6(4), keeping his year-end World No. 1 dream alive while Jannik Sinner remains sidelined.

The upsets kept coming: No. 23 seed Andrey Rublev crashed out to qualifier Rei Sakamoto 6-4, 6-4, and No. 6 seed Flavio Cobolli fell to 38-year-old journeyman Adrian Mannarino 6-1, 4-6, 6-3. Shanghai is chewing up seeds left and right.

And on a lighter note — Ben Shelton couldn't have asked for a better 24th birthday present, cruising past Daniel Altmaier 6-3, 7-6(6) to reach the third round in style.
