🎾 TENNIS NEWS YOU MIGHT'VE MISSED TODAY 🎾

Big news off the court first: the glitzy $6 million Six Kings Slam in Riyadh — Alcaraz, Djokovic, Zverev and Fritz were all booked in — has been pushed from October 21-24 all the way to December 27-30 after missile strikes disrupted Riyadh's international airport. A reminder that even the biggest exhibition paychecks in tennis aren't immune to the real world.

On the court in Shanghai, the men's year-end No. 1 race is basically academic at this point, but Alexander Zverev kept rolling anyway, beating Alex de Minaur 6-4, 6-4 to reach the semifinals. He'll face Alejandro Davidovich Fokina on Saturday for a spot in the final.

Over in Wuhan, chaos reigned. Top seed Aryna Sabalenka saved nine match points but still couldn't escape unseeded American Ann Li, who finally closed it out 3-6, 6-4, 7-6(5) for the biggest win of her career. Wild stuff.

Li's reward? A date with Iga Swiatek, who cruised past Ashlyn Krueger 6-1, 6-4 to book her own quarterfinal spot. Sonay Kartal, Jelena Ostapenko, Diana Shnaider and Maria Sakkari round out a stacked final eight in Wuhan.

Back in Shanghai, the last two quarterfinals go today — breakout star Rafael Jodar takes on Casper Ruud, and Daniil Medvedev faces Tommy Paul, with both winners joining Zverev and Davidovich Fokina in Saturday's semis.

And in bittersweet news for the home fans, freshly-crowned Beijing champion Zheng Qinwen had to withdraw from her OTHER home event, the Wuhan Open, with fatigue and a shoulder issue. Tough timing after the best title run of her career.
