🎾 TENNIS NEWS YOU MIGHT'VE MISSED TODAY 🎾

Two Asian swing title deciders wrapped up and a new Masters 1000 kicked off — it's a lot to catch up on. Here's everything you need to know 👇

Novak Djokovic is a China Open champion for a record seventh time, and it's the 102nd tour-level title of his career, after Alex de Minaur was forced to retire with a groin injury midway through the second set, having already dropped the opener 7-6(3). That puts Djokovic just ONE title behind Roger Federer's Open Era record of 103 — and he's now an absurd 34-0 lifetime in Beijing. At 39, the man simply refuses to slow down.

In Tokyo, Carlos Alcaraz went back-to-back at the Japan Open, outlasting Jiri Lehecka 7-6(7-4), 6-4 for his first title since a brutal four-month wrist injury layoff. It's a huge confidence boost heading into the back half of the season — and he's now the first man since Pete Sampras in 1994 to defend the Tokyo crown.

The women's side of the China Open saved its drama for today too. Iga Swiatek snapped Coco Gauff's 16-match winning streak in the semifinals, admitting afterward she finally "feels like I can play freely again." Waiting for her in the final: Liudmila Samsonova, who upset Elena Rybakina 7-6(7), 6-3 for her fourth straight win over the Kazakh — a genuine statement result.

And there's zero rest for the ATP — the Shanghai Masters, the last Masters 1000 of the year, got underway today, with Zverev, Alcaraz and Djokovic all handed byes as they wait to enter the fray.
