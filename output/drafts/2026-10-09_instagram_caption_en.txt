Shanghai is heating up and the title picture is getting clearer by the day — here's your catch-up on everything happening in the ATP and WTA worlds today.

The headline clash of the day: Novak Djokovic takes on former Shanghai champion Hubert Hurkacz in the second round. Djokovic leads their head-to-head 8-0 and arrives riding the high of his Beijing title run — but Hurkacz has beaten a king of this court before, and nothing is ever automatic in best-of-three tennis.

On the other side of the draw, top seed Alexander Zverev opens his own Shanghai campaign against home favorite Wu Yibing. With Jannik Sinner still out injured, every round Zverev wins keeps the pressure on for a possible year-end No. 1 takeover.

Carlos Alcaraz now has a name next to his on the draw sheet: Juan Manuel Cerundolo, who grinded past Colombian qualifier Nicolas Mejia 6-4, 6-4. Alcaraz arrives as the two-time defending Tokyo champion and will be looking to finally push past the quarterfinals in Shanghai, a round that's eluded him before.

And zooming out to the bigger picture — the race for Turin is taking shape. Alcaraz and Sinner have both already punched their tickets to the ATP Finals, but the scramble for the remaining six spots is still wide open, with Djokovic, Zverev and others all fighting for their season to continue into November.
